# Product steering

chat-ledger helps small sellers who run their shop in WhatsApp chats and get paid by
mobile money. It matches each order to its payment, flags suspicious confirmations, and
keeps an auditable double-entry record.

Principles:

- The seller's time is the scarcest resource. Every screen answers "what needs my attention now?".
- Explain every automatic decision in one plain sentence.
- Advice is advisory. The seller always has the last word.
- Works on a cheap phone and a slow connection: no heavy client bundle.
- The code, comments, commit messages, docs and UI strings are in English.
- Money is an integer in minor units. Never a floating-point number.
- Never use real phone numbers, real names, or real operator wording in the repository.
